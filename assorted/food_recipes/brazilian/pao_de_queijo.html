Pão de queijo (cheese bread)

Created at: 2026-04-20

Ingredients

  • 6 cups of tapioca flour
  • 1 cup of water
  • ⅔ cup of soy milk
  • ⅔ cup of rice bran oil (add an extra splash when mixing)
  • salt to taste
  • 2 cups of greated vegan cheese
  • 8 tablespoons of nutritional yeast
  • white pepper to taste
  • 1 cup of mashed potatoes
  • 1 teaspoon of smoked paprika

How

Simply mix everything in a bowl and kneed until consistent. Bake on 180C for 20-30 minutes or until golden brown.
