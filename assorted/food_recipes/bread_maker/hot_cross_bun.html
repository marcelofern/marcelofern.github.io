Hot Cross Bun

Dry:

  • 4 teaspoons of yeast (active)
  • 3 tablespoons of sugar
  • 4 cups of flour
  • ½ teaspoon of cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon of cloves
  • 1 teaspoon of allspice
  • 1 teaspoon of cardamom
  • 1 teaspoon of fennel seeds
  • Raisins or sultanas (as much as you like)

Wet:

  • ¼ cup of chia egg
  • 50g of butter softened (microwave) or oil
  • ½ cup of warm soy milk
  • ½ cup of warm water
  • Zest of 1 orange

Preparation

  • When the dough is ready, roll out into a rectangle.
  • On the top of the dough, add:
  • oil (neutral) or 25g of butter.
  • date syrup (or brown sugar).
  • more spices (cardamom, fennel seeds).
  • Pour onto dough + spread evenly.
  • Roll up the dough and cut into ~8 pieces.
  • Place the pieces in a baking tray lined with baking paper.
  • Bake at 180°C until ready (golden).
  • spread milk after 8 mins
  • (optional) pipe flour + water crosses on half way through.
