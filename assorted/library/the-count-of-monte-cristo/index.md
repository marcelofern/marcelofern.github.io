# The Count of Monte Cristo

```
Created at: 2026-08-02
```

This book took me by surprise. I thought it was just going to be a nice easy
read before bed, but it turned out to be a quite interesting story and ended up
as one of my favourite historical fiction books.

I didn't know the book would take so long, perhaps if I did I wouldn't have
started it. But it was very much worth it, the depth of details and variety of
characters throughout the book made me feel like I was going back to the 1800s.

The story is very compelling and has a satisfying ending, which I could
appreciate wasn't the "easy cope out" ending that I was expecting.

I also managed to watch the mini-series adaptation of the book from 2024, which
was very well done. They did have to condense multiple characters in one a few
times, and there was no personification of Count as other characters (Abbe and
the Lord), but I think the adaptation was quite good, and the differences were
subtle (apart from the ending) but well planned.
