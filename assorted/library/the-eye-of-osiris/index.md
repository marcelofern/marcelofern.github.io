I can't remember the exact reason why I set my eyes on this book but I have a
vague memory that I was looking for some "whodunnit" type of book and this one
came up.

It interested me because it is relatively old (1911) and the author himself
invented the "inverted detective story" genre that has inspired so many
detective and whodunnit books since.

The book is quite great and has good pacing. Not too fast that all the details
are missing or brushed off, also not too slow that you just want to skip pages.
The old English is also quite enjoyable to read and adds to the old-England
atmosphere of the book.
